ABOUT ELN

Connecting ambitious students with global opportunities.

Economic Leaders Network (ELN) is an educational platform created to help high school students discover opportunities in economics, finance, business and leadership.

Our Mission

Every year thousands of international opportunities become available for students, including competitions, research programs, internships, conferences and scholarships. Many students simply never hear about them. ELN was created to collect these opportunities in one modern platform so every student has equal access to information.

What We Offer

  • International Olympiads
  • Research Programs
  • Scholarships
  • Business Competitions
  • Summer Schools
  • Leadership Programs
  • Internships

Why ELN?

Instead of searching dozens of websites, students can discover verified opportunities in one place. ELN is designed to make finding opportunities simple, fast and accessible.

Who Is It For?

ELN is built for ambitious high school students who want to strengthen their university applications, participate in international programs and explore economics, finance and business.

Built by Students. For Students.

We believe that talent exists everywhere. Access to opportunities should too. Our vision is to create the world's leading student platform for economics, finance and business opportunities.
