ABOUT ELN

Building the next generation of economic leaders.

Economic Leaders Network is a platform created for high school students who want to explore economics, finance and business through real-world opportunities.

The Problem

Competitions, research programs, scholarships and internships are often scattered across different websites. Many students simply never discover opportunities that could help them grow.
